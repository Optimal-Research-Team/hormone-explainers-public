About this series

Education, not advice

These explainers describe how hormones work in general, adapted from the notes our clinicians teach from. They can’t tell you what’s happening in your own body.

  1. 01

    Adapted from clinical teaching

    Each chapter began as notes our clinicians teach from, and this public version was checked against clinical guidelines and published research.

  2. 02

    Evidence, labelled

    Where research is early or mixed, we say so. Prescription medicines are named for discussion with a clinician, never as a recommendation.

  3. 03

    Menopause care, when you want it

    Our clinicians put this thinking into practice in The Optimal Menopause Protocol. To talk it through, book an intro (opens in a new tab).
