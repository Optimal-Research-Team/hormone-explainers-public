    Your hormones as a tree

    This is a metaphor, a way to think about priorities, not a map of the body.

    Sex hormones are the canopy everyone notices, but they grow from roots and a trunk.

    Canopy · top line

    Testosterone, DHEA, estrogen, progesterone

    The hormones most people ask about. In this picture they sit on top of everything below, so problems lower down can show up here too.

    Trunk

    Insulin

    Carries energy up to the canopy. Insulin resistance is linked to fat stored around the middle, inflammation and shifts in the sex hormones above, such as lower SHBG.

    Roots · foundational

    Cortisol and thyroid

    Thyroid hormone and cortisol help set the pace of metabolism and the stress response in almost every tissue. In the metaphor, everything above draws on them.

    When the roots are unwell

    The canopy can suffer too. Treating only the leaves may not be enough if something lower down, such as untreated thyroid disease, poor sleep or insulin resistance, is also in play.

    That doesn’t mean canopy symptoms have to wait. Menopause symptoms, for example, can be treated directly and effectively.

    Look after the roots, too

    Support the foundations alongside any other care: regular sleep and daily rhythm, nutrition, movement and stress load.

    Thyroid conditions need diagnosis and treatment by a clinician, usually starting with a TSH blood test. If you have symptoms, ask to be checked.
